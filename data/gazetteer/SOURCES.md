object in `osm_ref` (OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). `uncertainty_m` is 150 for a specific
spot, 300 for a station or junction area, and 800 when the source names a whole neighbourhood.
Rows with a `check` note need a team member to confirm before the gold set is frozen.
